Ipoom Jeong is an Assistant Professor in the Department of System Semiconductor Engineering at Yonsei University. His research focuses on energy-efficient CPU and GPU microarchitectures, high-performance system architectures, near-data processing, and cutting-edge interconnect technologies such as CXL.
